All checkpoints under `Backend/results/` were **trained from scratch by this
project** on the synthetic reachability task described in README §4.

- No pretrained weights are used, downloaded, fine-tuned, or distilled.
- No BDH or BDH-CQ checkpoint was obtained or run. None is publicly available.
- Shipped checkpoint: 11,169 parameters, hash `a58f090f344e3f8b`, recorded in
